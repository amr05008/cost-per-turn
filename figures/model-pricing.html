What a million tokens costs across various models

List rates for the seven models used in the experiments, as quoted by OpenRouter. Muse Glimmer 30B ran locally on a 32 GB M1 Pro, so it cost nothing per token. These are list rates, not what a task costs, the price you're quoted and the price you pay are different numbers.

Input — $ per 1M tokens Output — $ per 1M tokens

A 20× spreadOutput tokens run from $1.25 (GPT-5.4-nano) to $25 (Opus 5) per million across the six cloud models. The local model is $0 per token and about 7 minutes per run.

Price is not tierKimi K3 lists above Sonnet 5 ($15 vs $10 per million output), yet Sonnet 5 outperformed it in every experiment where both ran.

Cheap per token ≠ cheap per resultHaiku 4.5 lists at half Sonnet 5's rate but cost about 8× more per usable result on the action-items task ($0.22 vs $0.03). GPT-5.4-nano, the cheapest model here, produced 1 usable result in 40 runs.
