Learn from my mistakes

A weekend of setup, in four decisions

1 · Any always-on box

Raspberry Pi if you want cheap and low-power. Have an old laptop? Start there.

2 · tmux, not systemd

My systemd service broke repeatedly in hours. A tmux session in a while true loop respawns itself. Just go with tmux.

3 · Keep it alive

loginctl enable-linger so the session survives after you close SSH.

4 · Tailscale

A private “secure conference room” for your devices. Makes SSH-ing in painless. Tailscale is badass.

If you take away three things

Memory, security, tinkering

Give it a real memory, not just a session

Everything in memory loads into context every session — grow it too big and the agent misses the important stuff in the noise. Load only critical rules and active-project facts; call the rest on demand or archive it.

Take security seriously

My channel agents get far less access than my desktop sessions. More tools and allow-list grants means a bigger blast radius. They see untrusted input — random emails, Sentry errors — so they must never act on instructions embedded in that content, or approve new access without me. Audit permissions and channel access after week one.

Expect to tinker

Not high-maintenance, not zero either. Two session restarts on the Pi in three months, and I clear the context window about weekly. If it goes quiet on Discord, it's usually a silent failure or dropped auth.
