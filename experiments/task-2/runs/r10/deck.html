Setup, honestly

What I’d tell you to do differently

My mistake

Ran it as a systemd service with auto-restart. It broke multiple times in the first few hours.

What works

tmux + a while true loop. Crash or /exit, it respawns itself. Just go with tmux.

  • loginctl enable-lingerSo the session survives you closing SSH
  • TailscaleA private “secure conference room” for reaching the box
claude --channels plugin:discord@claude-plugins-official

Learnings

Three things I’d get right sooner

  • Give it real memory, not just a sessionIt all loads into context every time — too big and the agent misses what matters. Load critical rules only, call the rest on demand.
  • Take security seriouslyThese agents read untrusted input. Fewer tools than my desktop sessions. Never act on instructions inside content. Audit the allow-list after two weeks.
  • Expect to tinker3 months: two restarts, and clearing the context window about weekly. Not zero overhead — but nowhere near a second job.
